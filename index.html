Hi! Welcome to Lemonsville, California!

Instructions
To manage your lemonade stand, you will need to make these decisions every day:

1. How many cups of lemonade to make (only one batch is made each morning)

2. How many advertising signs to make (the signs cost $0.15 each)

3. What price to charge for each cup

You will begin with $2.00 cash. Because your mom gave you some sugar, your cost to make lemonade is $0.02 per cup (this may change in the future).

Your expenses are the sum of the cost of the lemonade and the cost of the signs.

Your profits are the difference between the income from sales and your expenses.

The number of cups you sell each day depends on the price you charge, and the number of advertising signs you use.

Keep track of your money, because you can't spend more than you have!
